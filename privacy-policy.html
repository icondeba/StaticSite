Privacy Policy

Your Privacy Matters

This Privacy Policy explains how Success Paper Products, owner of the Successful© stationery brand, handles personal information collected through this website and direct business inquiries.

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Policy Sections

Scope Information We Collect How We Use Information Sharing Cookies and Links Retention and Security Your Choices and Rights Contact

1. Scope

This policy applies to information collected through www.successful.ind.in, our product inquiry channels, and direct communications with Success Paper Products about Successful© stationery products.

Our website is mainly an informational product catalogue for schools, retailers, distributors, institutional buyers and customers interested in stationery products. It does not currently process online payments or account registrations.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide

When you contact us by phone, email, map link, social media or any inquiry channel shown on the website, we may receive your name, phone number, email address, organisation name, city, inquiry details, product requirements and any other information you choose to share.

Technical and usage information

Like most websites, our hosting or browser-level services may process basic technical information such as IP address, device type, browser type, pages visited, referring page, date and time of visit, and general diagnostic information.

Third-party interaction data

If you open external links such as Google Maps, Gmail compose, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube or LinkedIn, those services may collect information under their own privacy policies.

3. How We Use Information

We use personal information for practical business purposes connected with your inquiry and our stationery supply operations, including:

  • Responding to questions, catalogue requests, quote requests and product inquiries.
  • Coordinating orders, dealer discussions, school or institutional supply requirements, and customer support.
  • Maintaining business, accounting, compliance and communication records.
  • Improving website content, product presentation, site reliability and user experience.
  • Protecting our website, business, customers and legal rights.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information only where needed for the purposes described in this policy, such as with internal team members, technology or hosting providers, email and communication providers, logistics or fulfilment partners, professional advisers, or public authorities where required by law.

When third-party service providers support our website or business operations, they are expected to use information only for the relevant service or lawful purpose.

5. Cookies and External Links

Our website may use essential cookies or similar browser technologies needed for normal site performance, security, diagnostics or embedded service behaviour. We do not use the site to run online payment checkout or registered customer accounts.

The website includes links to external services, including Google Maps, Gmail, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube and LinkedIn. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of third-party websites after you leave our website.

6. Retention and Security

We keep personal information for as long as reasonably required to respond to inquiries, maintain business records, complete supply-related communications, meet accounting or legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect legitimate business interests.

We use reasonable organisational and technical safeguards to protect information from unauthorised access, misuse, loss or disclosure. No website, email system or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be fully secure.

7. Your Choices and Rights

You may contact us to request access, correction, update or deletion of personal information you have shared with us, subject to legal, accounting and legitimate business-record requirements. You may also ask us to stop using your information for optional communications.

If you believe information you provided to us is inaccurate, outdated or no longer necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, please write to us using the contact details below.

For children or minors, a parent or lawful guardian should contact us for any privacy-related request.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, business process, legal requirements or contact channels change. The latest version will be posted on this page with the updated date.
